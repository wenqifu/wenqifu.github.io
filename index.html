Qifu (Danny) Wen

I study world models and model-based reinforcement learning, alongside efficient training and fine-tuning of large language models.

I am a Research Assistant at Shanghai Jiao Tong University. Previously, I worked in Boston University’s Department of Computer Science, advised by Prof. Reza Rawassizadeh. I hold an M.S. in Computer Science from BU and a B.S. in Mathematics from NYU Shanghai.

Applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.
